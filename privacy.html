Information handled by the tools

The site does not require an account. PicNotch processes selected images in your browser and does not provide an image-upload service. The winner tool places the names you enter in the fragment portion of the generated URL so the destination page can display them. Anyone who receives that URL can read those names, so do not use sensitive information.

The Metronome saves named presets only when you choose Save current settings. Preset names and settings are stored in this browser's local storage, not a site account. Use Delete in the preset controls or clear this site's browser data to remove them. Settings links contain tempo and practice options in the URL fragment, not preset names or a practice history. The Metronome does not access a microphone or record audio. Its optional screen wake lock requests that the device remain awake during playback where supported.

Advertising and cookies

All HTML pages include the Google AdSense script. Whether ads appear depends on Google's approval, account settings, and consent requirements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. The site also publishes an ads.txt authorization record; that record itself does not serve ads or set cookies.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google's partner sites policy. You can manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings. These settings do not replace consent where it is required.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted by Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps. As with most hosting services, technical request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps may be processed for delivery, security, and reliability.

External services

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts and libraries or icons from jsDelivr. The homepage also requests a sample photograph from Unsplash. These requests expose technical connection information, such as your IP address, to the provider; they do not upload the image you select in PicNotch. Requests to those services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies using browser settings. Blocking external resources may affect advertising, fonts, icons, or tools that rely on external libraries, but the tools do not require an account.
